# Record and review the evidence

Use this guide for the task's private requirement and evidence register. This register connects instructions to deliverables and results; it is working data, not a file to publish automatically. Use [Validation.MD](Validation.MD) to perform required checks and [Screenshot.MD](Screenshot.MD) for screenshot capture and review.

## Build one register

Start from the checklist in Instruction.MD. Use one row per assessable requirement, keeping separately required clients, automated/manual checks, and failure/repair stages in separate rows. Record rubric points only when they are explicitly supplied; do not predict a grade.

| ID and source location | Requirement and deliverable | Environment or client | Expected result | Observed result and source | Evidence location and version | Status and next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Task-specific identifier | One checkable obligation | Required application, client, or document | Expected outcome, or not applicable | Actual observation, who supplied it, or not observed | File/page/capture/log and relevant revision | Use a status below and state remaining work |

For document-only requirements, the observed result may be the inspected answer, formatting, or packaged file. Do not invent a runtime check for a requirement that has none. Add new rows when later instructions reveal additional requirements; preserve the reason for a changed interpretation.

For Word submissions, apply [Word.MD](Word.MD) and record the designated source file, original-preservation check, retention of every original part, and each required embedded picture's location in the final Word file. Track indented and justified prose answer paragraphs, black reflection/essay answer text, and retention of other worksheet colors as formatting checks. A standalone image's existence does not establish that it is embedded in the submitted document.

## Use statuses consistently

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Required work or evidence has not yet been obtained |
| Observed | A result or artifact exists but its adequacy has not been checked |
| Verified | The evidence was reviewed and supports this specific requirement |
| Mismatch | The evidence conflicts with an expectation or another record |
| Blocked | Missing access, tools, input, or a decision prevents this requirement from progressing |
| Not applicable | The requirement's stated condition does not apply, with the reason and source recorded |

An observed or supplied artifact is not automatically verified. Use the verified status only for the scope the available evidence supports. Mark other required aspects separately. Not applicable must not be used to hide a missing required item or an unapproved substitution.

## Establish what the artifact proves

Inspect the contents rather than trusting a filename, caption, checked box, or claimed pass. Distinguish an actual server response from a documented example; a test-runner report from ordinary program output; and one application's capture from another application's evidence.

Record whether evidence was captured by the user, captured by the agent with permission, or supplied from a historical task. Do not describe supplied evidence as a fresh execution or attribute an agent action to the student. If the origin or version is unknown, state that limit rather than inferring it from a timestamp or full-desktop frame.

Preserve the raw original and keep any allowed crop or redacted copy separately labeled. Evidence reuse across requirements is acceptable only when its visible contents support every linked requirement; each required client still needs its own proof.

## Reconcile before accepting

Compare the report, log, checklist, screenshots, data files, and any required Git evidence. Check that identifiers, results, application names, and versions agree. Treat a stale pending label alongside a result image as a discrepancy to investigate, not proof that either record is correct.

Check that each decision reason and validation statement belongs to its own question or defect. An explanation of a probable cause cannot stand in for an observed test result. Exported history and status are snapshots: identify the milestone they represent and do not claim they describe a later revision without checking.

Keep conflicting observations visible until resolved. Record what changed and why if later evidence resolves the conflict. Never invent a missing capture, rewrite a failure as success, or silently upgrade a historical claim to verified execution.

## Review privacy and close the register

Inspect text, standalone images, embedded images, exports, and requested repository history for personal data and secret values. Follow the original-file and redaction rules in Formatting.MD and Screenshot.MD. Privacy-safe evidence must still show the result being assessed; unresolved exposures prevent a ready-to-share handoff.

Pass the register to Submission.MD. Every applicable required row must be verified for a ready handoff. For partial work, list pending, observed, mismatched, or blocked rows with the exact next action and whether the agent or user must perform it.
