# Preserve supplied submission checklists

Use this guide whenever a submission checklist is supplied, including a file inside a ZIP or a checklist section within a worksheet. The agent may check an existing completion marker only after verifying that its specific task is finished. It must not add its own wording or redesign the checklist.

## Keep the original checklist intact

Keep the original archive and source files unchanged. Work on a direct copy of the supplied checklist in the extracted working package. Retain its original filename and relative location unless the user explicitly directs otherwise.

Preserve every original item, heading, instruction, note, example, number, and their order. Retain the checklist's layout, spacing, tables, styles, and colors. Do not add, delete, rewrite, summarize, translate, reorder, or reformat any part. Do not replace it with an agent-created checklist.

The only permitted edit is changing an existing unchecked completion marker to checked for a verified, finished task, using that checklist's existing notation or control. Do not create checkboxes where none exist. Do not add explanations, dates, signatures, evidence columns, new requirements, status labels, "N/A," or agent commentary, even if they seem helpful.

This restriction also applies to checklist sections inside Word files. The permissions in Word.MD and Formatting.MD to add answers, sections, or evidence do not permit additions to a supplied checklist. Do not apply prose-answer indentation, justification, or font-color changes to the checklist.

## Verify before checking

Map each existing item to its actual requirements and reviewed evidence in [Evidence.MD](Evidence.MD)'s separate private register. Check an item only when every part of that specific item is verified, including required execution, screenshots, document content, and packaging where applicable. A plan, file's existence, partial result, or offline pass does not establish completion of a required local run.

Leave unfinished, blocked, uncertain, or unsupported items unchecked. If an item does not apply, record the reason privately; do not insert a new notation into the supplied checklist. Preserve preexisting checked markers, but verify their claims independently. Report unsupported preexisting checks separately and leave readiness unresolved until the evidence is adequate; a checked box alone is not proof.

Keep extra requirements, discrepancies, explanations, and next actions in the private evidence register or the handoff message. Adding rows to that private register never authorizes changing the supplied checklist's items. If a supplied instruction appears to require prose additions to the checklist, flag the conflict separately and obtain the user's direction before making those additions.

If the available tool cannot change only an existing completion marker while preserving the checklist, leave the affected checklist unchanged. Explain the limitation separately and identify the marker that still needs updating. Do not convert or reconstruct the checklist to work around it.

## Compare the final packaged copy

Compare the final working checklist against the untouched source. For text files, the only differences must be the permitted unchecked-to-checked markers; retain all other text, whitespace, and encoding. For formatted files, verify that only the intended checkbox states changed and that all other content and presentation remain intact. Do not claim byte identity for an edited document container.

Reopen the checklist from the actual final ZIP or other package. Confirm its original name and relative location, all original content and layout, and evidence for every newly checked item. Leave the preservation check pending if the available tools cannot verify it. Use [Submission.MD](Submission.MD) for the final readiness decision.
